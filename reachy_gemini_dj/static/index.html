Connect your Google Account

Uses your own free Google Desktop App OAuth client. Takes about 5 minutes once.

  1. Open Google Cloud → Clients, make a project, then Create client → type Desktop app. Enable the Calendar, Tasks and Gmail APIs under APIs & Services if you want those.
  2. On the Audience page add your own Google address as a test user, or press Publish app so the sign-in does not expire after 7 days.
