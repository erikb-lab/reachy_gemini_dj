What it does

Everything a tabletop robot should do.

Say it out loud. Reachy handles the rest, and the control page on your phone covers anything you would rather tap.

360° photos

Reachy turns on the spot and stitches a full photo sphere on the robot. Five modes, from a 2-minute preview to 80 photos.

Music DJ

"Play some chill lo-fi." Streams, never downloads, ducks while you talk, and has a queue, favourites and an equalizer.

Moves and emotions

Pollen's emotion and dance libraries, plus poses you teach by hand and call by name.

Camera

"What do you see?" A local vision model describes the scene. Nothing leaves your network.

Weather and web

Free lookups through Open-Meteo and DuckDuckGo. No keys, no sign-ups.

Timers and alarms

Timers, reminders and wake-up alarms that survive a restart and ring until you answer.

Memory

"Remember our pit is A3." Facts live on the robot, and you can read and edit them.

FRC helper

Scan your Nexus QR code, hear queue alerts, and look up any team's history and results.

How it fits together

The robot stays light. The brain runs nearby.

Speech recognition, the language model and the voice run on a computer on your network, not in the cloud. Gemini Live is optional.

Reachy Mini

Runs the app: mics, speaker, camera, motors, the control page and the 360° stitching.

The brain

A laptop on the same Wi-Fi runs the free local models. First start warms up for a couple of minutes, then replies are quick.

Your phone

Open reachy-mini.local:8042 for volume, the gallery, push-to-talk, poses and settings.

Get started

Install in a minute.

Easiest: the app store

Open Reachy Mini Control, then Applications, then Discover Apps. Find Reachy Gemini DJ and press Install.

Or with the REST API

curl -X POST http://reachy-mini.local:8000/api/apps/install \ -H "Content-Type: application/json" \ -d '{"name": "reachy_gemini_dj", "source_kind": "hf_space", "url": "https://huggingface.co/spaces/sillymastermind/reachy_gemini_dj"}'

Then

Start the brain on a computer on the same network, open http://reachy-mini.local:8042, set Brain URL in Settings, and start talking. Try "play some lo-fi", "what do you see?" or "take a 360 photo".
